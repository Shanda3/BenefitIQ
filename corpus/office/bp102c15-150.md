## linked-care

Medicare can pay for dental care when it is needed so a covered medical treatment can go ahead. Examples include removing teeth before radiation for head or neck cancer, clearing an infection before an organ or stem-cell transplant, and heart valve surgery.

## care-coordination

The dentist and the medical provider must document that they coordinated this care. Without that written coordination, the dental care is not treated as linked to the medical treatment.

## implant-crown

A replacement implant or crown for a tooth pulled only to clear an infection is not payable.
