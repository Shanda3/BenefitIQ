## linked-examples

Covered examples include extractions before radiation for head or neck cancer, clearing an infection before an organ or stem-cell transplant, heart valve surgery, certain cancer treatments, and dialysis.

## care-coordination

The dentist and the medical provider must document care coordination. If that documentation is missing, the dental service is not paid as linked care.

## impacted-tooth

A routine extraction of an impacted tooth, with no linked medical treatment, is not covered.

## kx-icd10

From July 1, 2025, the dental claim needs modifier KX and an ICD-10 code. No specific diagnosis code number for these medical treatments is registered here.
