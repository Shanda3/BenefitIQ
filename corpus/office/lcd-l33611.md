## qualifying-sleep-test

A qualifying sleep test is required. Coverage of the appliance is limited to a diagnosis based on a Medicare-covered sleep test.

## practitioner-order

A physician, nurse practitioner, or physician assistant must see the patient in person and order the appliance. The dentist cannot order it.

## dentist-bills

A licensed dentist provides and bills the device.

## custom-device

Code E0486 is the custom device that these rules describe. Code E0485, a prefabricated device, is not payable.
