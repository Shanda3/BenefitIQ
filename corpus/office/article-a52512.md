## diagnosis-code

Diagnosis code G47.33 is the diagnosis code this policy article lists for the appliance.

## modifiers

This policy article names modifiers KX, GA, and GZ on the appliance claim.

## ninety-days

Fitting and adjustments for the first 90 days are included in the payment for the device. They are not billed on their own.

## five-year-replacement

Replacement is every 5 years.

## pdac-list

Code E0486 may be billed only when that device is on the Medicare product list for the code.
