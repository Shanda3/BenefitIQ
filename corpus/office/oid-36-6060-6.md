## who-is-covered

Oklahoma Insurance Department bulletin on section 6060.6 of Title 36, dated January 12, 2010. The bulletin says this section requires a health benefit plan that provides hospitalization benefits to cover anesthesia expenses, and hospital and ambulatory surgical center expenses, for a medically necessary dental procedure when the covered person is severely disabled, or is a minor eight years of age or under who has a medical or emotional condition that requires hospitalization or general anesthesia for dental care.

## expenses

The expenses named in the bulletin are anesthesia expenses and hospital and ambulatory surgical center expenses associated with the dental procedure. The bulletin does not name a code number. It does not say the dental procedure itself is the paid expense.

## no-diagnosis-required

The bulletin says the statute, as written, does not require a diagnosis of a medical, behavioral, or emotional condition before these anesthesia and facility expenses are covered. It says the statute refers to a child eight years of age or under who has an emotional condition that requires hospitalization or anesthesia.

## department-position

The same bulletin states the department's position that the intent is coverage of those anesthesia and facility expenses for a medically necessary dental procedure for a minor under the age of eight, taking into account the treating practitioner's judgment about anesthesia. A later official compilation of the statute was not added here. The court-network statute site disallows automated collection.
