Help

A check, not a claim

Start under New check or from an active procedure. Answer yes, no, or not sure. The result is one of three phrases: Bill medical first, Probably not medical, or Need more info. Nothing on this screen is sent to a payer.

The patient's plan is optional. Choosing one limits the sources to that plan, plus Medicare or SoonerCare rules when those rules apply. If this library has no policy for that plan and procedure, the page says our sources don't cover this for that payer yet and to confirm with the payer. If the criteria are licensed and not public, the page says so and does not guess.

How cases are grouped

  • Ready to bill. The answers matched a cited rule for billing medical insurance first.
  • Needs documentation. An answer was missing or not sure, or the chart is missing something the rule asks for.
  • Not covered or not found. The answers point away from medical billing, or the sources do not cover that line. Confirm with the payer.

Cards marked SYNTHETIC demo are examples. A check you finish is saved in this browser only. Clear my cases on the Cases page removes those saved checks. The database does not keep them.

Sources and codes

A line names a source and an effective date, or it says: Our sources don't cover this yet. Confirm with the payer. Code numbers use the placeholder labels in this app. Descriptor text for CDT and CPT codes is not shown.

The line on every screen

The banner at the top is part of the product, including the printed summary. It reads: Decision support for a human biller. Not a final coding or coverage determination.

The side panel labeled Ask about this case is off. It does not send a message. Advanced, at the bottom of the sidebar, opens the engineer view.
